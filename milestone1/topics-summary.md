# DX699 AI for Leaders — Weekly Lesson Topics Summary

Course: DX699 O1 AI for Leaders (Fall 26), Module B. Weekly rhythm: a graded quiz each week (5 pts, due Mondays
11:59am) + the weekly capstone reflection journal. Only Weeks 1–5 were published in the course shell as of
2026-09-26; Weeks 6–14 were not yet available.

## Week 1 — Introduction to Data-Analytic Thinking
- The Signet Bank case: data-analytic thinking in a real business setting
- Tukey's three trends driving data science
- Data science vs classical statistics
- Overfitting (oak vs maple example, the 21m cutoff)
- Key terms: churn, big data, data mining

## Week 2 — Business Problems and Data Science Solutions
- Nine data-mining task types
- Supervised vs unsupervised learning
- CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)

## Week 3 — Predictive Modeling, Part 1
- Features and targets
- Model parameters learned from data
- Entropy and information gain (housing worked example)
- Decision trees as building blocks

## Week 4 — Predictive Modeling, Part 2
- Classification trees
- Churn example (bill beats contract length as a predictor)
- Trees as nested IF/ELSE rules
- Instance space and decision boundaries
- Laplace correction
- Overfitting vs generalization

## Week 5 — Fitting a Model to Data, Part 1
- Linear discriminants (1D thresholds with probabilities)
- 2D linear combinations / logistic regression
- Inseparable points
- Margin
- SVMs: soft-margin and the kernel trick
