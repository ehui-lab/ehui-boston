# DX699 Weekly Self-Reflection — Requirements

## The journal template
- Official template: **"Capstone Development Reflection Journal.docx"**, attached under **Week 2** Blackboard materials
  (Module B: AI for Leaders).
- Title: "Capstone Development Reflection Journal", subtitle "Module B: AI for Leaders".

## Structure
- **Weeks 2–6** and **Weeks 8–11**: each week has two subsections —
  - `Assignment/Capstone work:` — what you did on the capstone that week
  - `Reflection:` — your reflection on the work
- **Week 7 - Milestone One** and **Week 12 - Milestone Two**: heading-only markers (milestone weeks)
- **Weeks 1, 13, 14**: no capstone assignments (footer note in template)

## Grading requirements
- **Minimum 5 weeks of entries** → worth 5 points ("Reflection: Weekly Journal")
- The journal is submitted as an **appendix** to the Milestone One paper — **not counted** in the 5–8 page limit
- Submission mechanism: save the journal to **Google Drive** and submit the **share link** alongside the milestone paper
  (not a file upload)

## What each week's reflection should cover
- The journal is mostly capstone project work rather than week-specific teaching content: each week advances the
  Milestone One paper (finding datasets, describing them, picking ML types, assessing data quality).
- Its tie to the week's teaching is a guiding question: **how did this week's material change your understanding of
  how data science impacts your industry of interest?** A short reflection on that suffices.
- Each week's entry = concrete capstone work done + honest reflection on what was learned/decided/changed.
